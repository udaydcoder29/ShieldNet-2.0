🔴 HIGH ALERT — UPI Fraud
₹2,800 Crore Lost to Cyber Fraud in First Quarter of 2025 — RBI Issues Emergency Advisory
The Reserve Bank of India has issued an emergency advisory as cyber fraud losses crossed ₹2,800 crore in Q1 2025. UPI-based scams account for 61% of all cases. The most common vector: fake "refund" requests that appear to come from legitimate bank numbers. AI-generated voice calls mimicking bank officers are now responsible for 23% of all cases.

🛡️ How to Protect Yourself — General Rules
🔒
Never share OTP
No bank, TRAI, or government agency will ever ask for your OTP, PIN or CVV over a phone call or message.
🔗
Verify URLs carefully
Real bank sites use .com or .in — never .xyz, .info, or hyphenated domains. Always check before clicking.
📲
UPI receive = you pay
A UPI "collect request" debits YOU. If someone asks you to approve a request to "send" money, it is a scam.
📞
Hang up on "officers"
Police, CBI, or TRAI do not arrest people over phone calls. Any such threat is 100% a scam. Hang up immediately.
💼
Jobs don't need deposits
Legitimate employers never ask for "security deposits", "training fees", or advance payments. Any such request is fraud.
🚨
Report on Cybercrime.gov.in
Report all cyber fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 (National Cyber Helpline) within the first hour for maximum recovery chance.
